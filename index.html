06 · Questions

Before you ask

Do I need an account to register?

No account, no password. Just your name, email and phone number on the event you want — that is the whole sign-up.

How many events can I enter?

As many as you can physically attend. Just avoid events that clash — check the date and time on each card before you confirm.

Can I cancel a registration?

Yes. Open My events and hit Cancel on any entry — your seat goes straight back to the pool for someone else.

What happens when an event is full?

Full events show a strikethrough and their register button is disabled. Follow the fest desk for waitlist announcements.

Ready to pick your events?

Seats go fast and the good ones always fill first. Scan the list and lock in your spot.
